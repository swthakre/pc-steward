# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and sharing private information without consent are not accepted.

Keep technical discussions relevant and evidence-based. Respect different experience levels and accept reasonable maintainer feedback.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Apply these actions consistently and proportionately.

For sensitive concerns, contact the repository owner through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish private information in an issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
